# CP-02 baseline protocol — private capture, public summary

**Purpose:** establish a defensible baseline of Salamat Mebel quote preparation without silently turning the brainstorm into software work.

## Sampling and measurement
- Sample consecutive inquiries over the agreed window; do not filter for won projects.
- Assign opaque IDs locally; never upload raw customer messages or personal details into public GitHub.
- Log timestamps in the same timezone and clearly mark missing observations.
- Compute median and spread of first-response and time-to-quote, quote delivery rate, and manual effort only from available measurements; show denominator and missingness.
- Segment by kitchen / wardrobe / other where material; note channel and complexity as possible confounders. Never infer causal sales lift from correlation.
- Record baseline process sketch, existing tools, alternative explanations, and permissions to use data.
- If logs do not exist, run a short prospective manual log instead of inventing history.
