# CP-02 — Salamat Mebel: inquiry → quote (case intake)

**Date:** 2026-10-16. **Owner selected this real process.** Status: INTAKE / PARTIAL; no observed results yet. MPE: EXTEND_EXISTING.
**Privacy:** public document contains process categories and empty fields only. Actual customers, phone numbers, message exports, deal values, costs, employee details and identifying evidence must remain outside the public repo.

## Process boundary
**Start:** customer contacts Salamat Mebel about custom case furniture (e.g. kitchen).
**End:** customer receives the first usable written quote / commercial offer.
**In scope:** lead arrival channel → initial response → requirements (dimensions, material, hardware, installation constraints) → any design/calculation handoff → internal estimate → quotation delivery.
**Out of scope for now:** advertising, production scheduling, procurement, manufacturing, installation, payments and unrelated CRM redesign.

## CP-01 seven-step application
1. Interest: existing furniture production and quoting experience (known context, not proof of customer pain).
2. People: prospective custom-furniture buyers; process owner is Salamat Mebel. Record which segment and channel actually dominates.
3. Behaviour: discover real lead steps and workarounds before assuming bottlenecks.
4. Problem hypothesis: waiting for sufficient information or calculation might delay quote and affect conversion. **Unverified**, competing explanations: high design complexity, low lead quality, client indecision, price differences.
5. Value alternatives: manual intake checklist, standardized estimate template, partial pre-quote; **automation is not the default**.
6. Experiment: not authorized until baseline and test design are reviewed.
7. Decision: NOT TESTED. No claim of saved time, higher sales, or causal conversion lift.

## Baseline data request (private, minimal)
Choose a contiguous recent interval (suggest 10–20 completed or abandoned inquiries, or 2 consecutive weeks, whichever yields a usable sample). Do not cherry-pick successes.
For each anonymized lead ID:
- inquiry timestamp; channel; first response timestamp;
- when critical dimensions/requirements became sufficient;
- when calculation began/completed; when written quote was sent (if any);
- reason not quoted / cancellation; complexity category; whether client later proceeded (if known);
- number of customer contacts and handoffs (if reliably logged).

Measure: inquiry→first response; inquiry→usable quote; request completeness; quote completion rate; hours of manual work; quote revisions; conversion only if traceable, with confounders noted. Treat missing timestamps as MISSING, not zero.

5. What would count as a meaningful result worth testing, and what effort/risk is unacceptable?
