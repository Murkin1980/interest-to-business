## Provisional findings
1. Market is significant in enterprise count but obtainable demand, CAC, payment readiness and willingness-to-pay remain unknown.
2. Local suppliers emphasize CRM, messenger integration, 1C, lead capture and deployment outcomes. This gives an initial *hypothesis* for differentiation: vendor-neutral diagnostic before choosing a tool.
3. Kazakhstan SME businesses require local source verification for tax, consent/privacy, payment, accounting, document exchange and RU/KZ output. No legal conclusion is provided.
4. International and local marketing offers should differ in examples, language, conversion channels and benchmarks; core method can be reused.
