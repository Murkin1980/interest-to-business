# Local market track — Kazakhstan-first

**Decision:** EXTEND_EXISTING. Independent research track within Interest to Business; no new repository, implementation, or deployment.
**Status:** CP-L01 preliminary desk research completed; source/offer validation and field interviews pending.
**Audience:** Kazakhstan first, Russia/Kyrgyzstan only later as separate jurisdictions.
**International:** Separate market positioning and English-language Product Hunt preparation only after product-market evidence; no duplicated codebase at this stage.

## Research checkpoints
- CP-L01 — Kazakhstan market, primary stats, payment realities, first provider scan.
- CP-L02 — Kazakhstan competitor/service offer matrix + 5–10 original SME interviews.
- CP-L03 — Russia and Kyrgyzstan comparison: payments, regulations, channels, pricing. No assumption of market equivalence.
- CP-L04 — Localization recommendations, acquisition experiments, economics, product gap assessment.

## Local adaptation hypotheses (not verified)
RU/KZ communication, messenger-led lead intake, accounting and document workflows, local payment methods, regulatory requirements and trust-by-referral may materially affect conversion. Confirm each with source evidence and customer interviews, not assumptions.

## Architecture
Universal discovery methodology belongs to Business Discovery; international and local go-to-market stay here as separate documents. Murat House owns public editorial content. Any future localized UI, connector, or data schema is subject to a new MPE filter and explicit deep-change approval.
